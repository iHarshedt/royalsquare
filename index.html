About Us

We Build Trust,
You Build Dreams

Royal Square Building Material Trading LLC is a leading UAE-based supplier of high-grade construction materials, grooved MEP fittings, chemical anchors, structural sealants, fasteners, and safety PPE. Operating from our central logistics warehouse in Jebel Ali, Dubai, we supply major infrastructure, commercial, and residential developments across all seven Emirates.

All materials comply rigorously with UAE Civil Defence, Dubai Municipality, British Standards (BS), ASTM, and DIN specifications. From site consumables and drywall finishing to high-pressure MEP installations, our technical team ensures seamless project submittals and rapid on-site fulfillment.

Talk to our team ↗
How do I request a quotation?

Use an “Enquire now” button or the contact form to open WhatsApp. Include product names, quantities, sizes, and your delivery location so our team can discuss your requirements.

Can I enquire about several product categories?

Yes. Send your full material list through WhatsApp or email, including any project specifications. Our team can review the items with you.

How do I confirm availability and delivery?

Contact our team with the required quantity, site location, and preferred date. Confirm current stock, lead times, and any delivery charges before placing your order.

What if my project requires a specific standard?

Include the exact standard, grade, dimensions, and documentation requirements in your enquiry. Ask the team to confirm the relevant product details before purchase.
